Protein is the one number that never moves. 200g is roughly 1.2g per pound of starting bodyweight. In a deficit it's the primary defense against losing tissue, and it's the lever with the strongest evidence behind it. Hit it on Sunday too — the zero-tracking day means no logging, not no protein.
Rate of loss ceiling — 0.75 lb/week. Faster than that and you're pulling from muscle, which defeats the entire point of running a recomp. Use a 7-day rolling weight average, never a single morning's number. If the trend is dropping faster than 0.75 lb/wk, add 150–200 kcal of carbs to lift days.

Premium Mobility Protocol
THU · 12–15 MIN
Unchanged from V4, but it now runs once a week instead of twice — the fifth lift day took Tuesday's slot. That makes Thursday's circuit non-optional, and moves 03, 05 and 08 worth appending to any lift day where you have five spare minutes. Sequence runs dynamic → static, spine → hips → neck → down-regulation.
01
Cat-Cow Flow
3 × 10 SLOW REPS
Segmental spinal movement — decompresses the lower back and wakes up the whole spine before anything else.
02
World's Greatest Stretch
2 × 5 EACH SIDE · 3 SEC HOLD
Hip flexor + t-spine rotation + hamstring in one flow. Covers more ground than almost anything else on this list.
03
90/90 Hip Switch
2 × 8 EACH SIDE
Hip internal/external rotation. Directly supports glute activation and counters anterior pelvic tilt.
Priority if short on time
04
Child's Pose to Cobra
2 × 8 SLOW REPS · 2 SEC PAUSE
Lower back decompression paired with gentle spinal extension. Undoes compression from rack pulls and RDLs.
05
Thread the Needle
2 × 6 EACH SIDE · 3 SEC HOLD
Thoracic rotation. Opens the upper back and takes pressure off the neck — critical for screen-heavy days.
Priority if short on time
06
Neck CARs
2 × 5 SLOW CIRCLES EACH WAY
Controlled Articular Rotations — full range through flexion, extension, rotation. Active control, not passive stretching. What actually protects the neck long term.
07
Standing Forward Fold with Sway
1 × 45–60 SEC · GENTLE SWAY
Passive hamstring and lower back release. Let gravity do the work — no forcing it.
08
90-Degree Wall Hip Flexor Stretch
2 × 30 SEC EACH SIDE
Direct anterior pelvic tilt correction. Back foot elevated against a wall or bench, hips pressed forward.
Priority if short on time
09
Supine Figure-4 Stretch
2 × 30 SEC EACH SIDE
Piriformis and deep glute release — often the hidden contributor to lower back tightness that stretching the back alone won't fix.
10
Deep Breathing Reset
1 × 2 MIN · KNEES BENT
Diaphragmatic breathing — 4 sec in, 6 sec out. Down-regulates the nervous system and lets everything you just mobilized settle in.

Operating Rules
NON-NEGOTIABLE
RULE 01
Double Progression — Still The Engine
Every exercise has a rep range. When you hit the top of the range on ALL sets with clean form, add weight next session. Log every session. Unchanged from V4, and more important now — the log is also your early warning system for the deficit being too aggressive.
RULE 02
Strength Is The Fat-Loss Guardrail
In a deficit, your logged lifts are the readout that matters most. Two consecutive sessions of regression on a key lift means calories are too low, not that you need to try harder. Add 200 kcal of carbs to lift days and reassess in a week. Body fat can come off slowly. Muscle you lose is gone.
RULE 03
Volume Is Now The Lever
V4's "two sets, brutally hard" was correct for a minimalist surplus. It is not enough when you're asking the body to build tissue while under-fed. Mostly 3 sets per exercise, 10–20 per muscle group per week. Progression still comes first — but the floor is higher now.
RULE 04
Effort: 1–2 RIR Compounds, 0–1 Isolation
Keep 1–2 in reserve on the heavy compounds — rack pull, dead stop press, leg press, RDL — where technique breakdown carries real injury cost. Take laterals, curls, extensions, calves and leg extensions to 0–1 RIR. That's where the last rep is free.
RULE 05
Cardio Is Unlocked
V4 capped Zone 2 because it ate the surplus. That constraint is gone. 30 min walk daily, 45 min Zone 2 Thursday, plus optional 15–20 min Zone 2 after any two lift days. HIIT is out — with five lift days in a deficit, it competes for the same recovery.
RULE 06
Protein Never Moves
200g minimum, every single day, including Sunday. Carbs flex with training, fat flexes with carbs, protein is fixed. If a day is going sideways, protein is the one number you still hit.
RULE 07
Deload Every 6th Week
V4 had no scheduled deload. Five lift days in a deficit makes one mandatory. Week 6 and week 12: cut sets by 40%, keep the loads identical. Same weights, half the work. Eat at maintenance that week.
RULE 08
Measure Composition, Not Weight
The scale is close to useless in a recomp — gaining muscle and losing fat can show as zero movement for weeks. InBody monthly, photos every two weeks in identical light, waist measurement weekly. Those three together tell you what's actually happening.
RULE 09
Sunday Is Still Sacred
One day a week, nothing gets measured. No macros, no sets, no reps. With five lift days on the board, the full nervous system reset matters more than it did before — sun, water, ground.
Medical oversight sits outside this document. Anything pharmacological — what you run, how much, for how long, and what comes after — belongs with a physician who is reading your bloodwork, not a training plan. Get baseline labs before week 1, mid-cycle labs, and post labs. If a marker moves the wrong way, that outranks every number on this page.
