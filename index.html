Fuel — Lift Day vs Off Day
190G PROTEIN · ALWAYS
Two templates, five lift days and two off days. Protein never moves. Carbs are the only real variable — they're high on lift days because they're doing a job, and low on off days because they aren't.
LIFT DAY · MON TUE WED FRI SAT
2,200 kcal — Maintenance
190g protein · 200g carbs · 70g fat. Not a surplus. You are asking the training to build tissue at maintenance, which is the entire premise of a recomp — the muscle-building capacity is elevated, so the calories don't need to be.
OFF DAY · THU SUN

190g protein · 115g carbs · 65g fat. Protein holds. Carbs come down roughly 40% because there's no session to fuel or refill. Thursday is tracked; Sunday runs on habit.

The honest math on this split: five maintenance days and two deficit days averages out to roughly 2,085 kcal against a 2,200 maintenance — about a 115 kcal daily deficit, or 800 per week. That is a slow burn from diet alone. Cardio is what makes the fat loss real in V5, not the calorie split. If the scale hasn't moved in three weeks, add cardio before you cut lift-day calories — protecting training quality on cycle is worth more than 200 kcal.

Operating Rules
NON-NEGOTIABLE
RULE 01
Double Progression — The Engine
Every exercise has a rep range. When you hit the top of the range on ALL sets with clean form, add weight next session. That's the entire system. Log every session — without the log, there is no progression, just exercise.
RULE 02
Effort: 1–2 Reps From Failure
Every working set ends 1–2 clean reps shy of failure. If you could have done 4 more, the set didn't count. The standard doesn't loosen because calories came down — training intensity is the signal that decides whether you keep muscle in a deficit.
RULE 03
Volume Comes From Frequency
Still mostly 2–3 working sets per exercise. The weekly volume went up because each muscle is now trained twice, not because any session got longer. Do not add sets to a session. If a group feels underworked, that's a conversation about the schedule, not the set count.
RULE 04
Thursday Has a Job
The one non-lift day is not "off." It's the week's longest Zone 2 block, the primary ab session, and the full mobility circuit — and it's the deepest calorie day. Skipping it isn't extra rest, it's the day the fat loss actually happens.
RULE 05
Sunday Is Sacred
One day a week, nothing gets measured. No macros, no sets, no reps. Full nervous system reset — sun, water, ground. Eat like an off day by habit, not by spreadsheet.
RULE 06
Fatigue Checkpoint — Tightened
Five lift days at maintenance is a narrower recovery margin than four in a surplus. If two consecutive sessions show strength regression, cut volume 30% for 4–5 days, sleep more, then resume. Regression is data, not failure — but at 5×/wk it's also the earliest warning you'll get.
RULE 07
Cardio Is Unlocked
V4 capped Zone 2 because it ate the surplus. There is no surplus now. Cardio is the primary fat loss lever in V5 — Thursday's anchor session, 3× post-lift Zone 2 (Mon/Wed/Fri), and the daily 30 min walk. Post-lift cardio still goes after lifting, never before, and never on a leg day.
RULE 08
Vacuums, Walk & Steps
3 rounds of vacuums + 30 min walk, every morning, fasted. 10,000 steps total for the day — the morning walk and post-lift/HIIT walking cover most of it, the rest gets filled by the evening walk. Not part of any lifting session, happens regardless of the day.
RULE 09
Protein Never Moves
190g every single day — lift day, off day, Sunday, sick day, travel day. Roughly 1.15g per lb. In a recomp this is the one number that carries the most weight, because it's what decides whether the deficit comes out of fat or out of tissue.
RULE 10
Judge by Body Comp, Not the Scale
A successful recomp can hold bodyweight flat for weeks. Monthly InBody scan + weekly progress photos + the 7-day weight average are the feedback loop. A single morning weigh-in tells you nothing and will talk you into changing a plan that's working.
RULE 11
Sleep Is Programming
7–9 hours, treated with the same seriousness as a set. Five sessions a week at maintenance means recovery is the binding constraint on the whole system — everything upstream of it is wasted if this is short.
RULE 12
Reassess Every Two Weeks
Weight trend, photos, and logged lift performance. If body fat is flat: add cardio before you cut calories. If lifts are regressing: sleep and calories come up before volume comes down. Change one variable at a time or you learn nothing.
Outside this document: compound dosing, ancillaries, estrogen management, and PCT structure are not in here and shouldn't be. Those are decisions that get made off bloodwork by a physician who is monitoring you — a sensitive estradiol assay (LC-MS/MS), a trough draw, lipids, hematocrit, and blood pressure. The training and nutrition in this document are built to be the part you control precisely. The medical part is the part you delegate.
